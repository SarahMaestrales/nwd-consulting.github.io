Teaching, Learning, and AI

A Practical Workshop for Teachers on Learning in the Age of AI

For Teachers All Subjects 175 € Saturday, December 12, 2026 9:00–13:00 NYC 15:00–19:00 Madrid

Stop getting slop-slapped

Tired of being slop-slapped with essays students generated? Learn how to make your students do the work again. Written by a research and assessment expert in the EdTech industry, this workshop shows educators how to leverage the weaknesses of AI to promote critical thinking. We'll discuss how to design assignments that encourage deeper thinking and make AI literacy a natural part of any existing course.

This course covers the basics of how LLMs work, how that influences their output, common mistakes to avoid when using AI, and—most importantly—how to put the effort of critical thinking back on the students. We'll explore ways to incorporate AI literacy into your courses by designing assessments around the tasks AI does poorly. Rather than creating isolated, subject-specific discussions, this shared workshop builds a stronger foundation for understanding how the math and statistics behind model development influence AI output across all contexts.

Workshop sessions

Five sessions covering AI literacy, common mistakes, critical thinking, and assessment design.

Session 1 · Teaching AI literacy

  • State standards and critical thinking competencies.
  • Tokens, prompts, and large language models: demystifying the jargon.
  • Learning how AI works.

Session 2 · Avoid these mistakes as an educator

  • Where AI-generated learning materials commonly fail.
  • Pattern recognition or construct mastery.
  • Problems with AI autograders.

Session 3 · Incorporating AI literacy into every course

  • How to talk about AI literacy in the context of any course subject.
  • Leveraging statistics to teach literature.
  • Critical source analysis.
  • Accuracy and precision.

Session 4 · A new age of assessment

  • Where LLMs are failing.
  • Leveraging AI's weaknesses to design assessments that AI can't do.

Session 5 · Stop getting slop-slapped

  • Bringing critical thinking back to the classroom.
  • Continuing the discussion on AI-proof assessments.

Takeaways that are yours to keep

  • A digital copy of the AI Literacy slides to use in your own classroom.
  • Less time grading AI slop.
  • A digital certificate of completion.
