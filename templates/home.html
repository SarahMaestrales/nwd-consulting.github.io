Services

No matter the scope, NWD Consulting Network is built to take your work further.

For academics and EdTech professionals we are ready to facilitate any stage of development. For early-stage EdTech start-ups and graduate students, we facilitate curriculum and assessment development, research planning, and data collection procedures. When things are already started, we provide expert content validation and professional review to ensure learning materials are accurate, culturally responsive, and properly aligned to standards. In later stages of development, our psychometricians and measurement specialists run the data to see where improvements could be made and prove the interventions work. And at the end stages, we facilitate technical writing and dissemination.

For businesses looking for "people science," we handle skills-based assessment for hiring, survey development, data analysis, and research design. Our psychometricians also build assessments for AI to see where layers are adding value and which agents can be replaced with better data collection. We also help navigate the cultural dynamics of international partnerships through product and pitch evaluations.

We also have services for students of all ages. We have tutors and guides ready to help with school work from learning to read through Advanced Placement Calculus and Physics. And for our graduate students, we advise on dissertation writing and research methodology.
