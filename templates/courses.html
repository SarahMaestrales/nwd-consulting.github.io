EMPLOYEE WORKSHOP

AI Literacy at Work

An AI Literacy Workshop for Businesses and Employers

An AI Literacy workshop developed for businesses to not only comply with the EU AI Literacy Act, but to also boost productivity. This workshop cuts through the hype to show what AI tools can and cannot do, and how to use them well in everyday work. Participants learn to spot where AI-generated material fails, to keep their own critical thinking in the loop, and to identify tasks worth automating, all while protecting their data and keeping costs under control. No technical background is required, and every session is built around real workplace tasks, so teams leave with habits they can apply the next day. The in-person nature of the workshop allows teams to share together examples they have uncovered in the workplace, identify repetitive tasks that could ethically be automated, and improve prompting strategies.

TEACHER WORKSHOP

Teaching, Learning, and AI

A Practical Workshop for Teachers on Learning in the Age of AI (optionally delivered 1 hour per week for 5 weeks.)

Tired of being slop-slapped with essays students generated? Learn to make your students do the work. This workshop was written by a research and assessment expert in the EdTech industry to show educators how leverage the weaknesses of AI to promote critical thinking. We will discuss how design assignments that promote critical thinking and turn AI literacy into a natural part of any existing course.

BEGINNER WORKSHOP

Boosting Job Skills with AI

A 6-Week Beginner Workshop in AI-Assisted Coding and Automation

Programming can feel inaccessible when even the instructions are full of unfamiliar words. This beginner-friendly workshop shows participants how to use AI as a guide through technical challenges, create simple digital projects, and build small automations with Python. No previous coding experience is needed.

Participants learn programming fundamentals, work with consistently formatted files, extract information from documents, and connect a Python script to an AI model. The final class brings these skills together in a practical project.

FAMILY WORKSHOP

AI, Social Media, and Growing Up

A 6 week Family Workshop on AI Literacy, Online Readiness, and Wellbeing

This practical, conversation-led workshop brings parents and students together to explore the science behind AI and social media, moving beyond the all-or-nothing hype often found in clickbait headlines. Families consider what research tells us about benefits and risks, where uncertainty remains, and how to make informed decisions.

Topics include online safety, social media readiness, mental health, social isolation, and the ways AI can influence thinking, memory, and sense of self. Families develop approaches that reflect their own values and circumstances.

STUDENT WORKSHOP

AI Literacy at School

A Workshop for Students on AI Literacy, Online Safety, and the Role of AI in the Classroom

It can be difficult to understand the reality of how AI works and how it can be used safely when faced with all-or-nothing clickbait opinion articles. This workshop is designed to help children understand when they can use AI for learning, when they should not, and the reasons why. Despite age restrictions, artificial intelligence (AI) and social media are an inevitable part of young people’s lives, influencing how they learn and communicate. We will explore the science behind AI and social media; their real-world impact on digital safety, and mental health, and learning; and how to use AI as a tool instead replacing their own voice and judgement.

This course can be tailored for students of different age groups and levels of ability. The course can also be modified to create examples relevant to specific courses such as math, statistics, science, history, political science, or literature.

COMMUNITY WORKSHOP

AI, Community, and Culture

A 6-Week Community Workshop on the Myths and Magic of AI

This six-week workshop brings together people of all ages to demystify artificial intelligence (AI), cutting through complex industry jargon and demonstrating that many of its concepts are already part of our daily lives. The goal is to foster a community-based, intergenerational reflection on how to coexist with these tools while maintaining one’s own independent judgment. The sessions range from the basic mechanics of large language models to philosophical conversations about the inevitable role of ai in society, ultimately offering critical thinking strategies for learning how to question and engage with AI in a conscious, informed way.
