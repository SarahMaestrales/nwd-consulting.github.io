COMMUNITY WORKSHOP

AI, Community, and Culture

A 6-Week Community Workshop on the Myths and Magic of AI

This six-week workshop brings together people of all ages to explore the myths and magic of AI. By looking behind the complicated language and uncovering what happens behind the scenes, participants can make AI more approachable and open up meaningful conversations across families, classrooms, and the wider community.

Topics include how large language models work, hallucinations and AI errors, AI's role in society, critical thinking, and evaluating AI-generated information.

TEACHER WORKSHOP

Teaching, Learning, and AI

A 4 hr. Practical Workshop for Teachers on Learning in the Age of AI

Tired of being slop-slapped with essays students generated? This workshop shows teachers how to help students think again by better measuring student understanding and using the weaknesses of AI to design assignments that promote critical thinking.

Educators explore ways to make AI literacy a natural part of existing courses while encouraging students to demonstrate their own understanding.

BEGINNER WORKSHOP

Boosting Job Skills with AI

A 6-Week Beginner Workshop in AI-Assisted Coding and Automation

Programming can feel inaccessible when even the instructions are full of unfamiliar words. This beginner-friendly workshop shows participants how to use AI as a guide through technical challenges, create simple digital projects, and build small automations with Python. No previous coding experience is needed.

Participants learn programming fundamentals, work with consistently formatted files, extract information from documents, and connect a Python script to an AI model. The final class brings these skills together in a practical project.

FAMILY WORKSHOP

AI, Social Media, and Growing Up

A 3hr Family Workshop on AI Literacy, Online Readiness, and Wellbeing

This practical, conversation-led workshop brings parents and students together to explore the science behind AI and social media, moving beyond the all-or-nothing hype often found in clickbait headlines. Families consider what research tells us about benefits and risks, where uncertainty remains, and how to make informed decisions.

Topics include online safety, social media readiness, mental health, social isolation, and the ways AI can influence thinking, memory, and sense of self. Families develop approaches that reflect their own values and circumstances.
