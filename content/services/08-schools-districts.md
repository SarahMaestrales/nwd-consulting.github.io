## What we do with schools

- **Teacher workshops.** [Teaching, Learning, and AI](/stop-the-slop/) is a practical workshop for teachers on learning in the age of AI. We also run [AI-literacy workshops](/courses/) for school communities and families.
- **Curriculum and assessment review.** Our curriculum specialists and psychometricians review materials and assessments for grade-level alignment, standards coverage, depth of knowledge and cultural fit.
- **Research and evaluation.** Research design, survey development and data analysis for school programmes and pilots, using de-identified data only.
- **Tutoring.** [Our tutors](/tutoring/) cover K–8 subjects, English language, the sciences, mathematics, writing and entrance exams.
