## Graduate Students 

NWD can help with research design for dissertation work. Should you use a nested model? What do you do if your results are non-linear? Our team has been involved in many domestic and international education research projects. Data analysts and psychometricians are prepared to help design survey and assessment instruments that will help you solve your research questions. Learn more about our [Research and Methodology Services](/research-methodology-data-analysis/).

NWD can also help ensure your results are correctly interpreted before you present. Did you adjust your p-values when conducting multiple tests? How do you talk about non-linear growth patterns? Why would you use a hierarchical model? We can help turn your findings into useful metrics for stakeholders or academic publications. Learn more about our [Writing, Review, & Dissemination Services](/writing-review-dissemination/).
