## Improve the Hiring Process

This is a service you probably don't even know you need.

Is it more important to you that a new employee already knows the software you are using, or is it better that they can adapt their existing knowledge to your framework and new settings? Do you want them to have the process memorized, or know how to use available resources to get the job done and check their work? Do you want your students to memorize facts, or apply those facts to explain unfamiliar phenomena?

Psychometrics is an entire field of science dedicated to measuring unobservable traits like knowledge, psychological attributes, satisfaction, or engagement. It is not just throwing questions on a sheet of paper. We research what is being measured, develop scales, test whether they are actually measuring what they say they will, and refine them by uncovering which questions are not contributing to the information collected by the scale. The goal is maximum quality and the shortest, cleanest scale possible. We test against other similar measures, validate the questions, and equalize tests for comparison.

Don't make the same mistakes as others who let AI make assessments without understanding the science behind assessment. Having AI write tests about AI-generated content runs the risk of an artificial feedback loop. All you know for sure is that students memorized what the AI thought was important.

NWD's psychometric experts can help you design task-specific assessments that test learning, adaptability, and growth. We can help you gather consumer data, including demographics, with community-informed research.
