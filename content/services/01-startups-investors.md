## Before You Invest

When everyone is pitching the same AI tutor, how do you know which one to back? 

NWD can help you make the difficult decisions. We have been rigorously testing LLMs and AI tutoring products to see where AI is working well, and where it is failing. We know what to look for. We have: content and curriculum experts ready to provide insights on the learning content and progression; psychometricians to show you how to tell if generated assessments are any good; gamification designers to say whether the incentivization will work for the intended audience; and more. 

We will let you know how the product compares to other tools on the market before you sign.
