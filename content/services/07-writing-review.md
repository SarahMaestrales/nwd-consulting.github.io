## Proofreading of Technical Writing

Have you ever delivered a perfect word-for-word translation only to be met with blank stares? Language and culture influence the jokes we tell, the way we tell them, and the phrases we use in everyday speech. Have NWD review your materials before publication or presentation.

NWD consultants are familiar with academic and technical writing. Many are STEM experts and published authors. They can facilitate your writing whether it is intended for business collaboration, academic audiences, employees, students, you name it.

Need someone willing to laboriously pore over the formulas to ensure the auto-formatting worked? Or to write a quick one-off script that will automate the formatting with AI? ## Dissemination of Research

NWD data analysts and psychometricians can help ensure your results are correctly interpreted before you present. Did you adjust your p-values when conducting multiple tests? How do you talk about non-linear growth patterns? Why would you use a hierarchical model? We can help turn your findings into useful metrics for stakeholders or academic publications.
