---
title: Privacy Policy
slug: privacy-policy
---

We do not collect data on visitors unless they fill out a webform. Our intake form is hosted by Google, and Google's own privacy policy applies to how it handles submissions. This site is published with GitHub Pages, and GitHub's privacy statement applies to that service.

Information entered into a webform will be used for only the stated purpose. We are not selling data, forcing subscriptions, or sending unsolicited messages.

If you enter your information into a webform to schedule a consultation, your information will be used to contact you for a consultation.

If you enter your information into a webform to discuss the monetization of your content, you will be contacted with more details regarding the upcoming platform and opportunities. If you are interested in continuing the conversation, I will save more details from our conversation with your permission.
