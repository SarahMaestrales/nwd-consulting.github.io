## AI, Community, and Culture

*A 6-Week Community Workshop on the Myths and Magic of AI*

The industry often uses complicated language to frame AI as some kind of wizardry, but many of these concepts are things we are already trying at home! This six-week workshop brings together people of all ages to look behind the smoke and mirrors, make AI more approachable, and open up space for hard conversations between parents and kids, teachers and students, and across the wider community.

Beginning with the basics of how large language models (LLMs) work, the workshop explores hallucinations and AI errors before opening the floor to community-led philosophical discussions about AI's role in society. The final two classes focus on critical thinking and evaluation, including how teachers can design more AI-resistant assignments and how we can question, challenge, and work with these tools without surrendering our own thinking.

## Teaching, Learning, and AI

*A 4 hr. Practical Workshop for Teachers on Learning in the Age of AI*

Tired of being slop-slapped with essays students generated? Written by a researcher in the education technology industry specializing in research and measurement, this workshop shows teachers how to help students think again. Educators learn how to better measure student understanding, leverage the weaknesses of AI, design assignments that promote critical thinking, and make AI literacy a natural part of existing courses.

## Boosting Job Skills with AI

*A 6-Week Beginner Workshop in AI-Assisted Coding and Automation*

Programming can feel inaccessible when even the instructions are full of unfamiliar words. This beginner-friendly workshop shows participants how to use AI as a guide through technical challenges, create simple digital projects, and build small automations with Python. No previous coding experience is needed. Participants learn to describe a problem, break it into steps, ask useful questions, and check that a solution works.

The workshop begins with using AI to navigate unfamiliar tools and understand how code becomes something a computer can run. Participants then learn programming fundamentals, work with consistently formatted files, extract information from documents, and connect a Python script to an AI model. The final class brings these skills together in a practical project addressing a problem from work, study, or everyday life.

## AI, Social Media, and Growing Up

*A 3hr Family Workshop on AI Literacy, Online Readiness, and Wellbeing*

AI and social media are becoming an unavoidable part of how young people learn, communicate, build relationships, and understand themselves. This practical, conversation-led workshop brings parents and students together to explore the science behind AI and social media, moving beyond the all-or-nothing hype often found in clickbait headlines. Families learn what research can tell us about the benefits and risks of these technologies, where uncertainty remains, and how to make more informed decisions about their use.

The workshop demystifies AI, explores why it sometimes gets things wrong, and considers how bots and online platforms shape digital experiences. It also opens conversations about online safety, social media readiness, mental health, social isolation, and the ways AI can influence thinking, memory, and sense of self.

Rather than prescribing one set of rules for every household, the workshop gives families the knowledge and confidence to ask better questions, evaluate risks, and develop approaches that reflect their own values, circumstances, and needs.
