AI is here to stay, but it doesn't mean the end of the world. NWD Consulting Network has designed a collection of four AI-Literacy workshops for educators, families, communities, and employees to step away from the all or nothing click-bait hype. We discuss what is AI, what it does well, what it doesn't do, and how to use it well in a variety of settings. 

1. Educators learn to design AI-Proof assessments that integrate AI literacy into any course. 
2. Families learn about online safety, social media readiness, how AI works, and what it is used for so they can make informed decisions when setting screen time boundaries.
3. Employees learn the very basics of installing python and creating simple automations to improve their workflow.
4. Community members come together to learn the science behind AI, how to leverage it's benefits, when it might not work as well, and open the floor for educated philosophical debate on the role of AI in their lives.

We talk about LLM homogenization a lot in the context of university level publication, but it is time to have the conversations about young authors losing their own voice to high-probability tokens; communities losing tourism because Gemini plans high-probability vacations; and businesses losing customers because the marketing is just so average that all the products seem the same.

If you are interested in hosting a workshop for you community center, school, or organization, you can [find out more about our workshops, here](https://nwd-consulting.com/courses/).
