Gordon Tai studied Psychology at HELP University, choosing the field because he wanted to work with people rather than spend his life behind a computer. He saw psychology as both a human and scientific discipline that helped him understand how people think, communicate, learn, and make decisions. More importantly, it gave him the foundation to influence behaviour ethically while remaining empathetic to people's experiences.
During university, he was heavily involved outside the classroom. He participated in coding clubs, Toastmasters, teaching initiatives, quiz bowls, and large-scale student events, eventually serving on the organizing committee for TEDxHELPUniversity. At the same time, he maintained strong academic performance, graduating with First Class Honours while studying on scholarship.

After graduation, Gordon entered the Learning & Development space as a Gamification Designer at Think Codex. There, he designed game mechanics and learning experiences for educational programmes. Although the work strengthened his instructional design skills, he eventually realised the role—particularly its focus on children's programmes—wasn't the right long-term fit. Feeling he wasn't contributing at the level he expected of himself, he made the decision to move on.

He later joined Alpha School in Austin, Texas, again as a Gamification Designer. This role shifted away from designing games themselves and towards building motivational systems, engagement campaigns, and behavioural frameworks that supported Alpha's two-hour learning model. His work focused on helping students make the most of their limited academic time through psychology-informed motivation and learning design.
Following that experience, Gordon moved into entrepreneurship. He founded Opus Cognitio, an instructional design and education consultancy that develops customised learning experiences, gamified programmes, and educational solutions for schools and organisations. Around the same time, he also co-founded an English education centre with his partner, where she led curriculum delivery while he managed business operations, marketing, customer acquisition, and strategic growth.
He later expanded into international commodities trading, working on transactions involving sugar, oil and gas, and related supply chains. The experience exposed him to commercial negotiations, relationship management, due diligence, Incoterms, and the realities of international trade, where persistence is often more valuable than a single successful deal.

More recently, Gordon returned to education through NWD Consulting, collaborating with a network of former Alpha School colleagues and education specialists. The consultancy supports schools and institutions by conducting educational assessments, curriculum audits, proposal development, instructional design, educational game creation, tutoring programmes, and AI-assisted learning solutions. One of its current initiatives explores AI-powered mathematics tutoring with a human-in-the-loop approach, reflecting Gordon's belief that technology should enhance—not replace—the compassion, judgement, and human connection that define great teaching.
Across every role, Gordon's motivation has remained consistent: to build practical solutions that genuinely improve people's lives. Whether through psychology, education, gamification, consulting, or even international trade, he enjoys solving complex problems, helping organisations grow, and empowering people to reach their potential.

His strongest interests lie in education, learning design, behavioural science, and communication. He is particularly passionate about helping students, educators, and institutions succeed through ethical, evidence-based approaches, and believes that the most meaningful innovation is innovation that remains deeply human.
