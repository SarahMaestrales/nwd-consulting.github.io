# Teaching, Learning, and AI

*A practical workshop for educators on teaching and learning in the age of AI*

Tired of reading essays students generated? Learn how to make your students do the work again. Written by a research and assessment expert in the EdTech industry, this workshop shows educators how to leverage the weaknesses of AI to promote critical thinking. We'll discuss how to design assignments that encourage deeper thinking and make AI literacy a natural part of any existing course.

## What we cover

1. **Teaching AI literacy**: standards and critical thinking competencies, and demystifying tokens, prompts, and large language models.
2. **Avoid these mistakes as an educator**: where AI-generated learning materials commonly fail, pattern recognition vs. construct mastery, and problems with AI autograders.
3. **Incorporating AI literacy into every course**: leveraging statistics to teach literature, critical source analysis, and accuracy and precision.
4. **A new age of assessment**: where LLMs are failing, and designing assessments that AI can't do.
5. **Stop getting slop-slapped**: bringing critical thinking back to the classroom.
